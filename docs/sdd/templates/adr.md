## Context

Describe the forces, constraints, and recurring problem that require an
architectural decision. Link to relevant specifications and existing ADRs.

## Decision drivers

- List the qualities and constraints that materially influence the decision.
- Keep drivers specific enough to compare alternatives.

## Considered options

### Option A: Name

Describe the approach, its advantages, disadvantages, risks, and operational
consequences.

### Option B: Name

Describe the approach using the same evaluation criteria.

## Decision

State the selected option and the decisive reasoning. This section must be
unambiguous when the ADR is `Accepted`.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Describe expected benefits.

### Negative

- Describe accepted costs, constraints, or new failure modes.

### Follow-up

- List required implementation, migration, documentation, or measurement work.

## Reversal strategy

Explain what would be required to replace this decision and what evidence would
justify reconsidering it.
